XRP Ledger Foundation

The XRP Ledger Foundation is an independent and nonprofit entity with a mission to accelerate the development and adoption of the decentralized XRP Ledger.
We collaborate with the XRP Ledger Foundation to shape new security standards for the XRP Ledger. As strong advocates of provable security, we are taking the XRPL consensus protocol the full distance, from pen-and-paper proofs to formal verification.
In addition, we maintain the Payment Engine specification, developed in collaboration with RippleX, ensuring it remains in sync with xrpld releases and monitoring changes to the code base.
