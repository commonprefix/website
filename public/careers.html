Careers

We're on a mission to solve the foundational scientific and engineering problems that stand in the way of mainstream blockchain adoption. We're starting by focusing on the areas of interoperability, scalability, and usability. But we can't conquer this frontier alone, we're therefore on the hunt for brilliant minds to join us.

At our core, we are a team of problem solvers and innovators. We embrace cutting-edge challenges across the web3 space and are deeply committed to the personal and professional growth of everyone in our team.

Open roles

  • Senior Software Engineer ↗ Remote or on-site in Athens, Greece · Full-time We're looking for a Senior Software Engineer to lead the design, implementation, and deployment of production systems that secure and move real economic value.
  • Head of Product ↗ Remote or on-site in Athens, Greece · Full-time We're looking for a Head of Product to own the product function end-to-end, deciding what we build, who we build it for, and turning deep research into products people actually want to use.
  • Auditor ↗ Remote or on-site in Athens, Greece · Full-time We're looking for auditors who can help us maintain the highest standards on security and reliability of the things we build.

If you don't see a suitable job opening but are still interested in joining our team, we'd love to hear from you. Reach out at careers@commonprefix.com or on X.

Why join us?

  • Impact

    We have a diverse set of partners, with many innovative projects. You'll have the opportunity to work on a wide variety of problems.

  • Great team

    We're a tight-knit team of researchers and engineers who are passionate about building the future of blockchain infrastructure.

  • Remote-first

    We're a remote-first company, so you can work from anywhere in the world.

  • Travel

    We are travel-friendly, from conferences to on-site visits. Get your passport ready!
